## 1. Brand foundation

### One-liner

Ascend AI builds AI automation around the way your business already works.

### Descriptor (under the logo, meta titles, JSON-LD)

Business Automation Solutions for small businesses

### 30-second pitch

Most small businesses run on the owner's spare hours. Enquiries answered at night, quotes chased at weekends, the same details typed into three systems. Ascend AI builds AI automation that takes that work off your desk. Not a software package you have to bend your business around. Systems built for how you actually operate, delivered in stages, and kept running by us.

### Voice rules

- Talk to one owner. "You", "your business", never "SMEs" or "organisations" in body copy.
- Concrete over abstract. Name the task (chasing a quote) rather than the category (workflow optimisation).
- Say what we do and what it costs. Owners have been burned by vague agencies.
- No numbers we cannot stand behind. No "save 20 hours a week" until a real client has.
- No hype vocabulary: seamless, transform, unlock, empower, leverage, cutting-edge, journey, streamline, tailored, revolutionise.
- Short sentences are fine. Fragments are fine. Mixed lengths always.
- British English throughout: organisation, enquiry, specialise.

### Words we use for ourselves

"Automation", "systems", "built around your business", "runs in the background", "we keep it running". Not "AI-powered", "intelligent", "smart", "solutions" in body copy (fine in the descriptor).

## 4. Solutions (`/solutions`)

**Meta title:** What We Automate | Ascend AI
**Meta description:** Enquiries, quotes, scheduling, customer questions, paperwork and reporting. See the kinds of work Ascend AI automates for small businesses, built around how you already operate.

### 4.1 Hero

**H1:** What we automate

**Sub:** These are the areas small businesses ask us about most. Yours might need one of them or a combination. The audit works out which.

### 4.2 Sections (one per area, same structure: what it is, what it looks like in practice, what stays with you)

#### Enquiries and follow-up

**The problem:** An enquiry comes in while you are on a job. By the time you reply, they have booked someone else.

**What we build:** Every enquiry, from your website, email, social pages or phone message, gets an immediate reply in your tone of voice. It asks the questions you would ask, works out whether it is a job you want, books a call or a visit, and follows up until there is an answer. You see every conversation and can step in at any point.

**What stays with you:** The decision. It qualifies and follows up. You still say yes.

#### Quotes and invoicing

**The problem:** Quotes take an evening to write and a week of nudging to get answered. Invoices go out late because they get done in batches.

**What we build:** Quotes drafted from your own price list and past jobs, sent for your approval or straight to the customer, then chased politely on a schedule you set. When the job is marked done, the invoice goes out and gets chased too. It connects to the accounts software you already use.

**What stays with you:** Pricing rules and the final approval on anything unusual.

#### Scheduling and reminders

**The problem:** Bookings taken by phone, written in a diary, forgotten by the customer.

**What we build:** Customers book, reschedule and cancel themselves within the rules you set. Confirmations and reminders go out by text or email. Your diary, your staff's diaries and your job list stay in step. No-shows get followed up automatically.

**What stays with you:** The rules. Who can be booked, when, for what, and how much notice.

#### Customer questions

**The problem:** The same twenty questions, every week, answered by whoever picks up.

**What we build:** An assistant on your website, email or messaging channels that answers from your own information: your prices, your opening hours, your policies, your product details. It only says what you have told it. Anything it cannot answer goes to a person with the conversation attached.

**What stays with you:** The source of truth. You change your information, its answers change.

#### Paperwork and data entry

**The problem:** The same customer details typed into three systems by hand, and one of them always ends up wrong.

**What we build:** Information captured once, from a form, an email, a photo of a document or a call note, then checked and pushed to every system that needs it. Job sheets, compliance records, supplier orders, onboarding forms.

**What stays with you:** Nothing you want to keep doing by hand. Anything that needs a human check gets one.

#### Reporting

**The problem:** You know the business is busy. You do not know if it is profitable this month until the accountant tells you.

**What we build:** The handful of numbers you actually run the business on, pulled from your systems and sent to you on the schedule you choose. Jobs booked, quotes outstanding, cash due, hours by staff member. Whatever matters to you.

**What stays with you:** The judgement. It shows you the numbers. You decide what to do about them.

### 4.3 What we do not do

**Heading:** What we don't do

- We don't sell a package and make you fit it.
- We don't replace the software you like. We connect to it.
- We don't put an AI in front of your customers that makes things up. It answers from your information or hands over to a person.
- We don't build something and disappear. See how it works.

### 4.4 CTA

**Heading:** Not sure which of these applies to you?

**Body:** That is what the audit is for.

## 5. How It Works (`/how-it-works`)

**Meta title:** How It Works and What It Costs | Ascend AI
**Meta description:** Free audit, fixed-price stages, then a monthly fee to keep it running. How Ascend AI builds and prices AI automation for small businesses.

### 5.1 Hero

**H1:** How it works, and what it costs

**Sub:** No long contracts, no big bang launch. Small stages, each priced before it starts, each live before the next one begins.

### 5.2 The process (four numbered steps)

**01. The audit**
A 30 minute call. You tell us how the business runs and where your time goes. We ask a lot of questions. Within two working days you get a short written document: the jobs we think can be automated, roughly what each would involve, and the order we would do them in. It is free and it is yours to keep, whether or not you work with us.

**02. Scope and setup**
If you want to go ahead, we scope the first stage in detail: what it does, what it connects to, what it will cost, how long it will take. You pay a setup fee, which covers getting access to your systems, the hosting and the groundwork everything else sits on.

**03. Build in stages**
Each stage has a fixed development fee agreed before it starts. We build it, test it with your real data, and switch it on. You use it for real before the next stage is scoped. Some businesses do one stage. Some do six over a year. You decide the pace.

**04. Run and improve**
Once something is live we host it, monitor it, and fix it when a supplier changes their system or your business changes its rules. That is covered by an ongoing monthly fee. Improvements and new stages are quoted as they come up.

### 5.3 How we charge

**Heading:** How we charge

Three parts, no surprises.

| | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup fee** | Access, hosting, foundations, the first detailed scope | Once, at the start |
| **Development fee** | Building each stage. Fixed price per stage, agreed before work starts. Larger projects are split into several stages, so this may recur while the build is underway | Per stage |
| **Ongoing fee** | Hosting, monitoring, fixes, support, small changes | Monthly, once something is live |

**Line under the table:** Exact figures depend on the size of the project. The audit document gives you a range before you commit to anything.

### 5.4 What we need from you

**Heading:** What we'll need from you

- An hour or two of your time per stage, mostly at the start, to explain how things really work.
- Access to the systems involved: your calendar, accounts package, website, inbox. We set this up securely and you can revoke it at any time.
- Someone to try it. Real use finds the exceptions that a demo never does.

### 5.5 Questions owners ask (FAQ)

**Will this replace my staff?**
Usually it replaces evenings and weekends, not people. It takes the repetitive parts off whoever is doing them, so they can do the work that needs a person.

**Do I have to change the software I use?**
No. We connect to what you already have. If something you use genuinely cannot be connected to, we will tell you at the audit.

**Who owns it?**
You own the automation built for your business. We host and run it for the monthly fee. If you ever want to move it elsewhere, we hand it over with documentation.

**What happens when something breaks?**
We are monitoring it, so we usually know before you do. Fixes are covered by the ongoing fee. If a supplier changes their system in a way that needs a rebuild, we quote that separately and tell you first.

**How long does a stage take?**
It depends on what it does and how many systems it touches. Most first stages are measured in weeks, not months. The scope tells you before you commit.

**Is my data safe?**
Your data stays in your systems. We access it with permissions you grant and can revoke. Where an AI model is used, we choose providers that do not train on your data, and we say which ones in the scope. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

**Heading:** Start with the audit

**Body:** Thirty minutes. A written list of what could be automated in your business. No obligation after that.

## 8. Legal pages

Both pages exist in full and are written for a venture studio that does not sell services. They must be re-drafted before the new positioning goes live, because the business is now a service provider processing customer and end-customer data.

### Privacy Policy: required changes

- Purpose of processing: enquiries, audits, delivering automation services, support, newsletter.
- Two roles: controller for our own contacts and subscribers; processor for client business data we access to build and run automation. A short data processing summary, with a full DPA available to clients on request.
- Sub-processors: hosting provider, email provider, AI model providers used in client systems. Named, with a commitment to notify changes.
- Retention: enquiries and audit documents (propose 3 years), client data (for the life of the contract plus a stated period), newsletter (until unsubscribe).
- Newsletter: what they get, how often, how to unsubscribe, lawful basis (consent).
- Cookies: currently essential only. If analytics are added in phase 2, a consent banner is required first.
- ICO registration number, once registered as a data processor.

### Terms and Conditions: required changes

- Replace the investment and partnership language with service terms.
- Audit: free, no obligation, the audit document is provided as-is.
- Engagement: scope documents per stage, fixed development fee per stage, setup fee, ongoing monthly fee, payment terms, what happens on late payment.
- Staging: each stage is accepted when live and used; how changes to scope are handled.
- Ongoing service: what the monthly fee includes and excludes, response expectations, how either side ends it, and handover on exit.
- Intellectual property: the client owns the automation built for them; Ascend AI retains its own tooling and methods.
- Third parties: AI providers and connected software have their own terms; Ascend AI is not responsible for their changes but will quote for fixes.
- Liability cap and exclusions, appropriate to a small service business.
- Governing law England and Wales, unchanged.

Both pages should be reviewed by a solicitor before publication. The blueprint provides structure, not legal advice.

5. **Registered address.** Include in the footer and legal pages or not.
6. **Enquiry type labels.** Confirm the five in section 7.3 or amend.
7. **Newsletter frequency.** Copy promises one email a month. Confirm or change.
