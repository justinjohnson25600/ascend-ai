Can I keep the software I use?

We build around the way you already work and connect to the software you use. The audit checks what's possible with your particular tools.

Do I still approve the important decisions?

Yes. You set the pricing, booking rules and approval points. Anything that needs a human check gets one.

What happens after it goes live?

We host it, monitor it and keep it running when your suppliers change their systems. Improvements are scoped as your business changes.
